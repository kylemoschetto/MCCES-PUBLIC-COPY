## Main Point 1: The Military Radio Frequency Spectrum

Per MCRP 3-40.3C Figure 1-1, the tactical spectrum is divided into three primary bands used by the Marine Corps. Each band has specific propagation characteristics and limits.

### Key Frequency Bands

| Band | Frequency Range |
|------|-----------------|
| **High Frequency (HF)** | 3 MHz to 30 MHz |
| **Very High Frequency (VHF)** | 30 MHz to 300 MHz |
| **Ultrahigh Frequency (UHF)** | 300 MHz to 3 GHz (3,000 MHz) |

---

## Main Point 2: Units of Measurement and Conversion

Frequencies are expressed in Hertz (Hz). To classify them accurately, operators must understand the relationship between different units to ensure they are comparing "apples to apples" in Megahertz (MHz).

### Unit Conversions

- **1 Kilohertz (kHz)** = 1,000 Hz
- **1 Megahertz (MHz)** = 1,000,000 Hz
- **1 Gigahertz (GHz)** = 1,000,000,000 Hz

### Conversion Rules

- To convert **kHz to MHz**: Divide by 1,000
  - Example: 3,000 kHz = 3 MHz
- To convert **GHz to MHz**: Multiply by 1,000
  - Example: 1.2 GHz = 1,200 MHz

## Practical Application

### Scenario

You are the Radio Chief for a distributed platoon. You are provided the following frequency list for the upcoming operation:
- 12.500 MHz
- 155.225 MHz
- 2400 kHz
- 1.2 GHz

You must categorize these to assign them to the correct radio nets (PRC-150, PRC-117G, or PRC-152).

### Steps

1. **Step 1**: Convert all frequencies to MHz
   - 2400 kHz → 2.4 MHz
   - 1.2 GHz → 1200 MHz

2. **Step 2**: Compare the MHz value against the limits in MCRP 3-40.3C Figure 1-1

3. **Step 3**: Label each frequency as HF, VHF, or UHF based on those limits

### Solution

| Frequency | Converted | Classification |
|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| 12.500 MHz | 12.5 MHz | **HF** (3-30 MHz) |
| 155.225 MHz | 155.225 MHz | **VHF** (30-300 MHz) |
| 2400 kHz | 2.4 MHz | **HF** (below 3 MHz threshold - verify) |
| 1.2 GHz | 1200 MHz | **UHF** (300-3000 MHz) |
